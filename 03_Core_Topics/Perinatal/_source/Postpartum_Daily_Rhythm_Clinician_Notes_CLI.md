**Layer context:** This template operationalizes L1 (sleep, medication adherence) and L2 (regulatory predictability) into a concrete, fillable tool. The anchor-point model replaces rigid scheduling because perinatal L2 must accommodate infant unpredictability. The weekly check-in section provides structured self-monitoring across all four layers without using layer language.

**How to use at discharge:**
1. Fill out Section 1 (shift schedule) and Section 3 (medication) together with the patient and partner at the discharge planning meeting
2. Help them identify 4-5 realistic anchor points in Section 2 — push back gently if they set too many or too ambitious ones
3. Have them start the support network map (Section 4) and complete it within the first week home
4. The weekly check-in (Section 5) should be brought to every outpatient appointment for the first 3 months

**Clinical red flags in the weekly check-in:**
- Mood rating of 1-2 for 2+ consecutive weeks → medication reassessment
- "Not at all" on leaving the house for 2+ weeks → social isolation intervention
- "I'm worried" on baby connection → assess for bonding disorder; consider dyadic therapy referral
- Partner rating of 1-3 for 2+ weeks → partner depression screening (PHQ-9)
- Sleep block not happening → troubleshoot barriers immediately; this is the #1 intervention
