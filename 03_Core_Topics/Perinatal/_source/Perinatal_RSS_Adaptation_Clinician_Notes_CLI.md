*(This section is for clinical staff — not for families)*

**Layer context:** This guide addresses all four RSS layers with perinatal-specific modifications:
- **L1:** Sleep architecture intervention via shift parenting; medication selection per LactMed/Briggs database; thyroid screening mandate; psychosis screening
- **L2:** Anchor-point model replacing rigid scheduling (accounts for infant unpredictability); 3-month planning horizon
- **L3:** Partner role drift monitoring; intrusive thought normalization and differential diagnosis (harm OCD vs. command hallucinations); extended family management
- **L4:** Graduated return-to-function timeline; work re-entry as clinical decision; social isolation as treatment target

**Sequencing mismatch warning:** The most common perinatal sequencing error is jumping to L3 (bonding interventions, couples therapy) before L1 is stable. A parent who hasn't slept more than 3 hours in a stretch cannot participate in relational repair. Fix the sleep first. Always.

**Contraindications:**
- Do not use this guide for parents with active psychosis — psychosis requires a different clinical pathway
- Do not use this guide as a substitute for screening (Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale should be administered per protocol)
- Not appropriate for stillbirth or neonatal loss — those require grief-specific materials

**Adaptation notes:**
- For single parents: modify partner references throughout; emphasize community supports, postpartum doulas, and respite care
- For LGBTQ+ families: language is gender-neutral but verify pronoun preferences; chosen family members may be primary support
- For adolescent parents: layer on pediatric/adolescent materials; school re-engagement is an additional L4 consideration
- For parents with SUD history: add dual diagnosis overlay; medication selection avoids abuse-potential agents
