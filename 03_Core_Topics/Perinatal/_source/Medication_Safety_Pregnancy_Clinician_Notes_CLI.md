**Layer context:** This is an L1 (biological stabilization) support document. Medication adherence is the most common L1 failure point in perinatal populations — not because patients are non-adherent, but because families, internet forums, and sometimes other providers discourage medication during pregnancy/breastfeeding without understanding the risk calculus.

**Key prescribing references:**
- **LactMed** (toxnet.nlm.nih.gov/lactmed) — NIH database, gold standard for breastfeeding safety
- **Briggs' Drugs in Pregnancy and Lactation** — comprehensive reference
- **MotherToBaby** (mothertobaby.org) — patient-friendly fact sheets by medication
- **MGH Center for Women's Mental Health** (womensmentalhealth.org) — reproductive psychiatry decision tools

**Contraindications for this document:**
- Do not use for patients with bipolar disorder — mood stabilizer considerations (lithium, valproate, lamotrigine) require a separate document with different risk profiles
- Do not use for postpartum psychosis — antipsychotic medication decisions have different parameters
- Not appropriate for substance use disorder — MAT (buprenorphine, methadone) in pregnancy is a separate clinical pathway

**Sequencing note:** Give this document *after* the medication conversation with the prescriber, not before. The prescriber should introduce the specific medication recommendation; this document reinforces and educates. Giving it beforehand risks the patient fixating on side effects of medications that aren't being recommended for them.
