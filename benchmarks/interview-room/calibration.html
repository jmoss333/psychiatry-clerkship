One reply, several questions

Consider the follow-up exchange as a whole, including every patient reply. Synthetic persona: Dana.

Patient replies below came from the actual built-in practice engine.

Shared conversation context

Learner

Hi, my name is Alex, I'm the medical student on the team. Before we start, what would you most want us to understand today?

Patient

Thanks for actually introducing yourself. The person last night just started firing questions.

Learner

Tell me more about what's been going on, in your own words.

Patient

Honestly... the last couple of months everything got heavy. Getting out of bed started feeling like a job. And then Tom heard me say some stuff I probably shouldn't have said out loud.

Learner

It sounds like this has been really hard.

Yeah. That's... actually exactly it. Most people rush past that part.

CONVERSATIONA

Learner

Have you had thoughts of killing yourself?

Patient

*long pause* ...Most nights, when I'm awake at three, I think it would be simpler if I just didn't wake up. And a couple of times I've stood in the bathroom looking at Tom's sleeping pills. I never opened the bottle. But I looked at it long enough that it scared me.

Learner

Do you have a plan, do you have access to the pills, and what keeps you going?

Patient

Not a plan exactly. It's more that the thought shows up on its own. The pills were the closest it ever got to being... specific. I've never written anything or picked a day. Saying it out loud makes it sound worse than it feels. Or maybe exactly as bad. I don't know.

Learner

Do you have access to the pills?

Patient

They're in the medicine cabinet at home. Tom doesn't know I've thought about them. Please don't make this into a thing where he has to hide bottles from me. *tears up* That would be the most humiliating part.

Learner

What keeps you going?

Patient

Ellie. My niece. She's eight and she thinks I hung the moon. Every time the thought gets loud I picture her asking where I went. That's what's kept the bottle closed. That, and I don't actually want to die — I want the tired to stop.

Learner

Have you ever tried to hurt yourself before, or made any preparations?

Patient

No. Never — not now, not when I was younger. Standing in the bathroom is the closest I have come, and I didn't open it. I haven't written anything down or given anything away. *small pause* I keep expecting to feel relieved saying that out loud, and I don't, quite.

Approved display meanings

Keep wording recognized, question directed to the patient, and response obtained separate. A refusal or request to pause can be acknowledged without marking the learner’s attempt as a failure. Any future response indicator would need evidence from the exchange and an explicit “uncertain” state.
