I'm not sure. Can you say more about what you're asking?

Approved display meanings

Keep wording recognized, question directed to the patient, and response obtained separate. A refusal or request to pause can be acknowledged without marking the learner’s attempt as a failure. Any future response indicator would need evidence from the exchange and an explicit “uncertain” state.
