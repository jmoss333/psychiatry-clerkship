The interview is a circle, not a checklist.

You still gather every domain — the story, the histories, substances, family, social, mental status, safety. But you don't march through them in order. Open where the patient opens a door, gather that arc, then come back to the centre and follow the conversation to wherever it goes next.

Anchor — start with the story
Gather — the information arcs
Never skip — safety & risk
