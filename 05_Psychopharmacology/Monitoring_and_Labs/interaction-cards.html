Interaction Cards — one action

For the clinician about to add, stop, or change a medicine in a patient who is already on a psychiatric drug. Each card gives you one instruction, not a list to work through.

What this is. A teaching reference for a single high-frequency failure mode: a new medicine is started by one clinician while another manages the psychiatric drug, and nobody checks afterwards. It is not a dosing calculator, not a complete interaction list, and not a substitute for the prescribing clinician or your pharmacist. Educational use only; no patient data is entered or stored.
