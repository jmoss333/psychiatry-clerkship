# Local policy questions for an APP psychiatry fellowship pilot

No local answer is supplied in this document. It is an intake checklist for the institution. For every answer, record the policy title, version/effective date, institutional owner, authorized professions and settings, source location, review cycle, and whether the material may be exposed on a public learner site.

## Scope and supervision

- Which assessment, examination, ordering, prescribing, consultation, emergency, discharge, and procedure tasks may a PA fellow perform in each pilot setting?
- Which of those tasks may a PMHNP fellow perform in each pilot setting?
- Which limitations arise from license/certification, credentialing/privileging, assignment, payer rules, local policy, or supervisor judgment? Keep these categories separate.
- Who is the named supervising clinician for each shift/clinic; who covers; how are they reached; when must the fellow pause; what happens when supervisor and fellow disagree?
- Which changes in task, setting, or responsibility require a new approval rather than being inferred from time in program?

## Clinical operations and escalation

- Who owns pending and missed tests, serial results, contact attempts, medication access barriers, cross-coverage, and follow-up after transfer?
- What are the authorized local pathways for urgent medical assessment, behavioral emergencies, restraint-related work, withdrawal complications, capacity consultation, involuntary processes, and disposition?
- Which focused physical or neurologic examination tasks are expected, in what setting, and under what supervision?
- For transitions, what constitutes recipient acceptance and when does responsibility transfer?

## Medication workflows

- Which prescribing and medication-management actions require direct review before action?
- Who owns monitoring, missed monitoring, pharmacy coordination, prior authorization, administration scheduling, missed visits, and medication reconciliation across transitions?
- Which product-specific labeling, pharmacy procedures, and local protocols are current, and who maintains them?
- Which medication-switching or deprescribing examples may be used for education, and what wording must remain nonoperational or dose-free?

## Documentation, billing, telehealth, and records

- What note types, sign/co-sign requirements, amendments, supervision statements, and consult conventions apply to each profession and setting?
- Which payer/date/setting-specific billing and coding rules, if any, are in pilot scope and who from compliance approves teaching material?
- What telehealth identity, location, privacy, emergency, disconnection, documentation, and cross-jurisdiction rules apply?
- Where are real supervisor observations and formative/summative evaluations recorded? The website will not receive them.

## Program operations

- Who selects and orients preceptors, verifies qualification, schedules repeat observations, and assures coverage?
- What protected time exists for mentoring, case review, faculty development, and learner support?
- What is the pilot population, duration, host surface, release authority, incident path, and stop criterion?
- Who decides whether real-data work is quality improvement, research, or another governed activity?
