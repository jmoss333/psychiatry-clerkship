# Faculty review packet: role, supervision, observation, and feedback

Status: source-linked draft for review; not learner content. Clinical review owner: unassigned.

## Gap IDs

G01–G07, G40–G42.

## Educational questions

1. Does the optional PA/PMHNP bridge framing help learners choose preparation without treating profession as a proxy for ability?
2. Which help-seeking and uncertainty behaviors should the first three activities prepare for?
3. Which behaviors require real observation, who is qualified to observe them, how often should they recur, and where does the institution record feedback?
4. How should private rehearsal remain clearly separate from institutional evaluation and entrustment?

## Proposed draft material

The implemented software presents two optional starting bridges, three shared workplace tasks, and the sequence **prepare independently → rehearse here → arrange observation**. The only private choices are Revisit, Discuss with my supervisor, and Try another case. There is no score, pass/fail label, readiness threshold, competence label, supervisor record, or authority unlock.

## Generalizable statements versus local decisions

Generalizable for faculty consideration: preparation, structured feedback, repeated practice, mentorship, and qualified preceptors are recurring themes in the rechecked standards. Institution-specific: actual task permissions, supervisor availability, pause/escalation criteria, observation cadence, evaluative standards, record systems, and release authority.

## Faculty decisions required

- Accept, revise, or reject each bridge name, summary, and resource sequence.
- Define the observable behaviors for the three initial tasks without importing MS3 OSCE score-to-entrustment mappings.
- Decide whether a blank observation prompt should be developed and what clinical wording it may contain.
- Decide what small pilot could evaluate without claiming effectiveness or readiness.

## Institutional decisions required

- Supply the current role/setting scope matrix, supervisor/coverage map, observation staffing, record destination, and escalation process.
- Name the production host and pilot population.
- Decide whether any evaluation is formative, summative, or both; keep it outside the website unless an approved institutional system is explicitly integrated in a later project.

## Rights and open questions

No proprietary assessment item is reproduced. Before adopting any external rubric or instrument, verify permissions. Open question: which accreditor, if any, the institution intends to pursue; do not represent compliance or accreditation before that decision and formal review.

## Conditions for learner release

Faculty disposition on route framing and selected resources; institution-owned scope/supervision/observation decisions; privacy review; successful software/accessibility verification; named pilot and rollback authority. A merge alone does not meet these conditions.
