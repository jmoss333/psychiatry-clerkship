## Generalizable statements versus local decisions

Generalizable question: can the learner recognize uncertainty, explain what a result does and does not establish, and prepare a focused request for help? Local decisions: who performs which examination, who orders/interprets/acts, whom to contact, urgency, documentation, and transfer of responsibility.
