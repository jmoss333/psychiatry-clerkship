## Proposed draft material

The collateral/transition activity card separates independent preparation, rehearsal, and arranged observation. A future scenario may reveal a late access or acceptance barrier, but no operational case, timing rule, billing answer, legal conclusion, or clinical feedback is authored here.

## Generalizable statements versus local decisions

Generalizable faculty question: does the learner make uncertainty and unresolved work visible, communicate through an appropriate channel, and seek confirmation? Local decisions: note ownership, co-signature, consult acceptance, legal authority, recipient acceptance, timing, payer rules, emergency operations, and publication of internal links.

## Faculty decisions required

- Author a bounded consult/handoff and transition scenario with source-linked explanatory feedback.
- Define what behaviors an observer can describe without converting the website into an assessment system.
- Decide whether billing, legal, or telehealth content belongs in the first pilot at all.

- Decide whether internal policy titles/links may appear on a public learner site.
