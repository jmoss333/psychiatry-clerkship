## Proposed draft material

The medication activity card links canonical preparation resources and states that rehearsal should prepare a supervision question. ## Generalizable statements versus local decisions

Generalizable design question: can a learner connect a plan to monitoring, changed information, uncertainty, communication, and confirmed next responsibility? Local decisions: authorization, product availability, ordering, review timing, missed-result workflow, contact attempts, pharmacy/payer steps, administration, cross-coverage, and documentation.

## Faculty decisions required

- Select the first bounded case and its exact evidence sources.
- Author dose-free findings, options, explanatory feedback, and one-detail change.
- Decide which content is product-specific and where specialist/pharmacy review is mandatory.
- Decide whether any existing monitoring text needs separate correction before reuse.

- Provide current pharmacy, authorization, supply, missed-visit, handoff, and escalation procedures.
- Identify qualified observers and the approved record system.

## Rights and open questions
