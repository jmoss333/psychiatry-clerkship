# Aftercare packet: the owner's directions for PR #1036 (design before code)

**Status: draft design. PR #1036 stays a draft and `FD_CARE_PACKET_ENABLED` stays `false`. Nothing here is approval
for patient use or activation.** Gate G-C (ward printer, a nurse, a peer specialist) stays open; activation (G-D) is a
separate decision.

This document turns the owner's directions into a design that can be reviewed before it is built. Where a direction
leaves a choice open, the choice made here is marked **[chosen]** and listed at the end for the owner to confirm. Nothing
marked **[chosen]** is a clinical judgement: patient-facing wording is changed only where a direction requires it, and
each change is listed for review.

## 1. The directions (verbatim from the owner)

1. Aim for a measured five-sheet default without shrinking text or cutting safety instructions. Make the callback,
   family, general helpline directory and insurance pages opt-in. Keep the core crisis guidance. Show me the resulting
   page count before we finalize defaults.
2. Exclude withdrawn, closed and invalid-contact listings from the chooser and all output. Use verified crisis resources
   in the dedicated crisis block; don't mix them into routine aftercare selections. Reuse the shared eligibility rules
   rather than creating another version.
3. Require a verified unit phone for a Final copy that names the unit or promises contact. Never imply the unit provides
   24/7 crisis coverage. Drafts can show an explicit missing-contact warning.
4. Don't hard-code Maine Medical Center as approved branding. Make the organization/site line configurable and keep
   institutional branding approval pending.
5. Make the wallet card a separate, optional single-sided print. Keep duplex behavior unapproved until we test it on the
   ward printer.
6. Clear packet should clear user-entered unit details and reset every confirmation. A verified configured unit profile
   can be loaded again explicitly.
7. Use the official "988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline" name from the validated data. Resolve the name-versus-label contract
   consistently across schema, renderer and tests.
8. Leave the older print workflow unchanged while this remains off. Consolidate only after the new packet passes review
   and is separately activated.
9. Rebase and assess #1008 separately. Automated axe checks will help, but don't replace keyboard, screen-reader or
   real-print testing.

Also: CI auto-fix only for scoped technical corrections proposed for review (no automatic merges, clinical wording
changes, flag activation, weaker tests, looser thresholds or suppressed security alerts); fix the reproducible
wallet-card overflow and investigate the CodeQL findings; reconcile the #1034 dependency before calling #1036
merge-ready; rerun the full checks on the final combined revision; keep G-C open.

## 2. What the investigation found (2026-10-12, read-only)

- **Eligibility already lives upstream.** The directory feed (`/care-directory.v1.json`) is generated by ReConnect
  (`scripts/care-directory/feed.cjs`, with a frozen snapshot of the shared record-status and phone rules from ReConnect
  draft PRs #1882 and #1880). It drops closed and withdrawn records, private and blocked-public records and three
  quarantined names, and blanks a rule-blocked phone or website. `categories[].excluded` counts what it dropped. The
  Clerkship repo has no eligibility rule of its own; `fdCareDirectoryValidate` is structural only. **So the packet must
  consume the feed's verdicts, never port the rule into the browser.** A browser copy would be a third version.
- **What the packet cannot see:** why a contact is empty (blocked by the rule, or never on record). It can see that no
  action survived (`contactHref` and `website` both empty: 182 of 5,684 live listings, 89 of them recovery meetings whose
  location and day are not projected).
- **Gaps in the packet today:** a crisis-category listing can be added and prints under "Crisis services" (112 of 126
  would print); a listing with a registered crisis number in its name or phone is blocked only at print, after the
  trainee has built the packet (9 non-crisis, 14 crisis); a listing with no usable contact can print as name and area
  only; the sheet prints a phone's text even when the shared rule refused to make it dialable.
- **Wording:** one real 24/7 implication, in the core welcome text, paragraph 4: "Reach out to us or to a crisis line,
  any time." Contact promises with no hours: welcome paragraph 3 ("call the unit ... and we will help"), the whole
  callback page, medicines bullet 1 ("call the unit. We can help." with no token, so no gate can see it), medicines
  bullet 4, and the rights page (off by default). The welcome also names the unit (sign-off, header), so under direction 3
  every Final copy needs a verified unit phone.
- **Page budget (estimates from recorded heights, to be re-measured in Chromium):** the owner's set (welcome + first
  week + warning signs + medicines; callback, family, helplines, insurance and the card out) is 4 / 5 / 6 sheets at
  4 / 8 / 12 listings. Making the first week opt-in as well gives 4 / 5 / 5. The tray already suggests eight or fewer
  listings.
- **Dependency:** #1036 contains #1034's two commits unchanged and merges into main cleanly today. A merge-commit merge
  of #1034 needs no work in #1036 (the repo's norm for `claude/*` PRs); a squash or rebase makes #1036 conflict on 12
  files (resolved by keeping #1036's side, verified to give the same tree). CodeQL's one alert on #1034 is the same
  `/<script>/` line #1036 already fixed.
- **Governance:** none of #1036's paths is content or governance today. `care-inserts.v1.schema.json` is governance;
  `care-inserts.v1.json` is neither. A schema edit plus a data edit in one diff does not trip L1 (verified on a
  hypothetical tree). Schema edits go in their own commit and are called out for the owner.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 Eligibility (direction 2)

One pure function, `fdCarePacketEligibility(row, payload)`, in `fd_care_packet_sheet.js`, returns `''` for an eligible
listing or a reason code. **[chosen]** A listing is not eligible when:

| Reason | Condition | Source of the verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `CRISIS_CATEGORY` | `row.category === 'crisis'` | the owner's direction; the dedicated crisis block comes only from `payload.contacts` |
| `CRISIS_NUMBER` | `fdCarePacketCrisisHit` on its name or phone | the governed record, through the payload (already the sheet's rule) |
| `NO_CONTACT` | no `contactHref` and no `website` | the feed: both are non-empty only when the shared permission rule allowed the action and the shared phone and URL validators passed |

Withdrawn and closed need no packet code: the feed does not ship them, and a saved id the complete feed no longer lists
already drops.

- It runs at **every entry point**: the chooser's button, "Add this page" and "Add all results", `toggle`, `addMany`,
  the tray, the sheet, and the copy text. The controller keeps a cached **packet view** of the directory (records
  filtered by the function, recomputed only when the feed object changes) and uses it for everything the packet shows.
  Search still lists every record (it is a directory, not an output).
- An ineligible result has **no Add button**, and says why in one short line ("Crisis services print in the crisis box,
  not in this list." / "No working phone or website is on record." / "Its number is a crisis line, which prints only in
  the crisis box.").
- The sheet **also** refuses an ineligible listing that reaches it (defence in depth; blocker `INELIGIBLE` naming it),
  and prints a phone only when `contactHref` is present (an undialable number is not printed; a website-only listing
  prints its domain and code).
- Not done here **[owner's call]:** blocking verification-due listings (2,034 live; the shared rule is notice-only), a
  stale-snapshot print blocker, projecting address and meeting time for in-person services, and the crisis-block
  freshness window (the five governed lines cross ReConnect's 90 days on 2026-10-25).

### 3.2 Unit identity, verified contact, draft warning (directions 3, 4, 6)

**A unit profile is data, validated at build time, loaded explicitly.** `care-inserts.v1.json` gains an optional
`unitProfiles` array (default empty, and **empty as shipped**: branding approval is pending). A profile is
`{id, label, org, brandingApproved, unitName, unitPhone, callbackProgram, phoneConfirmedOn, phoneConfirmedBy}`.

- The validator requires a ten-digit North American number that is not N11 and not a registered crisis number,
  `phoneConfirmedOn` a real past date (warn when stale), `phoneConfirmedBy` a role (no name or patient text), `org` and
  `unitName` free of doses, phone numbers and crisis numbers. **A profile that is in the payload is, by construction,
  confirmed.** The payload ships `{id, label, org, brandingApproved, unitName, unitPhone, callbackProgram}` and strips
  the confirmation record (it is internal, like the helpline rows' dates).
- **State:** `unit = {name, phone, profile}`. With `profile` set, name and phone come from the payload and the fields
  are read-only with a "Use my own details instead" button (which clears the profile and starts from blank). Typed
  values are **user-entered and unconfirmed**.
- **Org line [chosen]:** printed only from a loaded profile with `brandingApproved === true`. Otherwise no org line. The
  `FD_CARE_PACKET_ORG` constant is removed.
- **Clear packet:** clears the listings, the mode, the page switches, **the typed unit details and the loaded profile**,
  and resets every confirmation. A profile is loaded again only by the explicit control.
- **The rule [chosen]:** a Final copy **names the unit or promises contact** when any of these is true: the header names
  the unit or an org; any rendered text contains `{{unitName}}` or `{{unitPhone}}`; the callback page is on. Such a
  Final copy needs a **confirmed unit phone, which only a loaded profile provides**. Otherwise the blocker is
  `UNIT_UNCONFIRMED` with a message that says to load a profile or print a Review copy. Because the welcome page itself
  names the unit and promises a call to the unit's number, **every Final copy needs a loaded profile**. As shipped (no
  profile) a Final copy is therefore unavailable until the owner adds a confirmed profile; Review copies work. This
  follows directly from the direction and is stated in the PR body.
- **The callback page [chosen]** promises a call from the unit. In a Final copy it additionally needs the loaded profile
  to record `callbackProgram: true` (blocker `CALLBACK_UNCONFIRMED`). Whether the ward runs a call-back program is an
  operational fact only the owner can supply.
- **Draft warning:** a Review copy with no confirmed unit phone prints an explicit line in the header, in ink
  (never colour): "Draft: the unit's phone number is not confirmed yet." The same line is shown in the preview. The
  wording avoids "verified" (the sheet forbids that word on paper).
- **Never imply 24/7 unit coverage [chosen, wording for the owner's review]:**
  - welcome paragraph 4 becomes "If you feel worse instead of better, do not wait. Use one of the lines in the box
    below; they answer any time, day or night." (the five governed lines are all 24/7 in the data);
  - medicines bullet 1 gains the token it lacks ("call the unit at {{unitPhone}}") so the confirmed-phone rule can see
    it;
  - a **validator rule** (so it cannot regress): a sentence that refers to the unit (`us`, `we`, `our`, `the unit`, a
    unit token) may not also carry an availability phrase (`any time`, `anytime`, `24/7`, `24 hours`, `day or night`,
    `around the clock`, `all hours`); and any sentence containing `the unit` must contain a unit token;
  - not changed, proposed for the owner: welcome paragraph 3, callback bullets, medicines bullets 3 and 5, the rights
    page, and the safety page's "care team" (see the investigation report).
- Typed unit details remain allowed in a Review copy (draft use), so the packet can still be tried on the ward.

### 3.3 Defaults and page budget (direction 1)

Data change only: `callback`, `family`, `national`, `insurance` become `defaultOn: false`; the card leaves the packet
pages (3.5). Defaults then: welcome (core), first week, warning signs, medicines. **[chosen]** First week stays on (the
owner's list does not name it). The browser then **measures** the default packet in both copies at 4, 8 and 12 listings
and the result is shown to the owner **before** defaults are finalised; the first-week-off variant is measured too.
Nothing about type size or safety content changes to reach the count.

### 3.4 Official crisis names (direction 7)

The renderer prints `payload.contacts[ref].name`, the governed resource name (already shipped and byte-pinned to
`crisis_resources.json`), in the crisis block, on the card and in the copy text. `crisis[].label` is retired: the schema
makes it optional with a description that says it is not rendered, the data drops it, and the validator and tests stop
requiring it. The schema's rendering-contract text is rewritten so the schema, the renderer and the tests say the same
thing (name and contact and availability from the governed record; `when` from the data). The schema edit is its own
commit. A later PR can delete the property.

### 3.5 The wallet card (direction 5)

The card is not part of the packet pages. It is a separate **Print wallet card** control in the preview that prints one
page containing only the card, with its own staged container, its own crisis-line check (every required line, using the
governed names) and its own blockers. The preview says: "Print the card by itself, one-sided. Printing it two-sided has
not been tested on the ward printer." Nothing claims duplex works. The packet's pages no longer carry the "print
one-sided" hint. The card must fit ID-1 (3.375 x 2.125 in) with headroom under a wide face (Verdana reproduces the CI
runner exactly), with the official names. Layout is chosen after prototypes are measured (see section 5).

### 3.6 The older print workflow (direction 8)

No change to `fd_care_pack.js` or its Print path. A test pins that, with the flag off, the new packet registers no
print listener, stages nothing and changes nothing on the older workflow. Consolidation is a later decision.

### 3.7 #1008 (direction 9)

A separate task: bring the axe branch up to date with main by merge, run it, and report what it covers on the packet.
No claim that it replaces keyboard, screen-reader or real-print testing.

### 3.8 CI, CodeQL, the dependency

- Wallet-card overflow and the three CodeQL alerts are fixed (commit 8ddb7837); the card fix is also re-verified after
  the card moves out of the packet pages.
- #1034 gets the same one-line CodeQL fix on its own branch, then is merged into #1036 by a plain merge. #1034 is merged

# Revision 2: changes after independent review (2026-10-12)

Three independent reviews (clinical safety on the strongest available model, governance and architecture,
accessibility and testability) and three measured wallet-card prototypes attacked section 3. Everything below supersedes
the matching part of section 3. Items marked **[owner]** are not settled by engineering and are listed in the PR.

## R2.1 Changes adopted

**Safety and wording**
- **Final-copy availability must not turn the Review copy into the take-home page.** The Review copy's printed promises
  ("we will finalize this together", "We will print a final copy ... before you leave", "We will keep what you tick for
  the final copy") become "we will go over this with you before you leave" wording that does not depend on a Final
  copy existing. The unit-unconfirmed message says a Final copy needs a confirmed unit profile and that none is set up;
  it does not steer to the Review copy. **[owner: wording]**
- **Activation is coupled to a profile.** The build fails when the packet flag's source literal is `true` and the
  profiles file has no profile. As shipped (flag false, no profile) nothing changes.
- **Welcome paragraph 4** (the one real 24/7 implication): "If you feel worse instead of better, do not wait. Call or
  text one of the numbers in the box below. They answer any time, day or night." (no semicolon; true for each of the
  five governed lines in at least one mode; the validator now requires each referenced line's governed availability to
  begin "24/7" while the welcome makes that claim). **[owner: wording]**
- **Medicines bullet 1**: both "call the unit" sentences gain the `{{unitPhone}}` token (mechanical; the confirmed-phone
  rule can then see them). **[owner: wording]**
- **Validator rule** (so none of this regresses): a sentence that refers to the unit (word-bounded `us`, `we`, `our`,
  `the unit`, a unit token) may not carry an availability phrase; any string containing `the unit` must contain a unit
  token; titles and headings are exempt from the token requirement. The rule is run against the current data and what
  it flags is listed.
- **The paper draft line** appears only when no unit phone is filled in: "Your nurse will write the unit's phone number
  on this page before you leave." A typed but unconfirmed number prints with no paper line (a "not confirmed" line next
  to a printed number contradicts it); the preview says the number is not confirmed. The line is static, after the page
  title, bold with a rule, never colour. **[owner: wording]**
- **The preview reads the typed phone back** ("Patients will be told to call (207) 555-0100.") so a transposed digit is
  seen before printing.
- **Crisis-category exclusion stays**, but the PR body names what it removes (the Maine peer-support warmline and the
  Maine and national domestic-violence and sexual-assault advocacy lines, among others) so the owner can decide whether to
  add governed helpline rows or ask ReConnect to recategorise. **[owner]** No helpline rows are added: a number cannot
  be confirmed from here.
- **Defaults**: the owner's list is applied as written. The page report shows the owner's set, the owner's set with
  "Making home safer" on, and the variant that trades the first-week page for it, at 4, 8 and 12 listings, in both
  copies, measured in Chromium. The clinical review's view: a means-safety page outranks the first-week planner.
  **[owner]**

**Trust and architecture**
- **Unit profiles live in their own file**, `care-unit-profiles.v1.json` (shipped as an empty list) with its own
  schema, validated by the existing care-inserts validator. A profile is `{id, label, org, brandingApproved, unitName,
  unitPhone, callbackProgram, phoneConfirmedOn, phoneConfirmedBy}`. The payload ships
  `{id, label, org, brandingApproved, unitName, unitPhone, callbackProgram, usableThrough}` where `usableThrough` is
  `phoneConfirmedOn` plus 180 days (a fixed date, so the build stays reproducible). **The browser refuses a Final copy
  after `usableThrough`** (a service worker can serve an old payload; a tab can stay open for days). The confirmation
  record itself is not shipped. Profiles apply to both learner sites.
- **The claim is stated honestly:** a profile in the payload carries a confirmation record that was reviewed at merge.
  The repo's governance machinery does not stop one PR from loosening the validator and adding a profile; the owner may
  want the profiles file and `validate_care_inserts.py` added to the governed list and an owner-side code-owner rule.
  **[owner]**
- **Browser-side re-check:** every profile value is run through the unit-name and unit-phone validators when the payload
  is read; a failing profile is not offered. One rule for a unit phone in both the validator and the browser (ten
  digits, area code and exchange start 2-9, neither a service code, not any registered crisis number, not any
  helpline-table number), pinned by a parity test.
- **One resolver**, `fdCarePacketUnit(state, payload, now)`, returns `{name, phone, org, confirmed, callbackProgram}`
  for the sheet, the card, the copy, the preview, the blockers and the announcements. State carries `unit.profile` (a
  bounded id) through copy, save and normalize; with a profile set, typed name and phone are dropped; lookups use a
  linear scan; an id not in the payload is "not loaded".
- **Crisis numbers:** the payload gains `crisisNumbers`, a digits-only list built from the validator's own
  `crisis_numbers()` (every governed resource, `contact` and `alsoAvailable`), so the browser sees every registered
  number. A parity test reads the governed record at run time.
- **Eligibility has one choke point.** `fdCarePacketEligibility(row, payload)` is applied in the packet's
  id-level helpers, and the sheet receives the FULL feed plus the payload and checks the saved ids against the
  function. Two different outcomes: *missing from a partial feed* stays hidden (kept); *present but ineligible* is
  blocked with a named reason (`INELIGIBLE`), listed in the tray with its own Remove button, and counted in the add
  results. A saved id that has become ineligible is never silently dropped. When the payload is not usable, every
  listing is ineligible with one reason shown once. Counts for "Add this page" and "Add all results" cover eligible rows
  only, with a visible note when some were not eligible.
- **A phone prints from the feed's dialable contact:** the number is shown only when `contactHref` exists, formatted
  from it, with "Call" for `tel:` and "Text" for `sms:`; the free-text phone is not printed on its own.
- **Copy as text** is blocked only by what the copy contains (listing and crisis blockers). The unit and callback
  blockers apply to Print, not Copy, because the copied text names no unit and promises no contact.
- **Older print workflow while the flag is on:** the packet stages its print only when the print comes from the packet
  (its Print button, or the print command while focus is inside the packet), never when the older pack's Print button
  starts it. Flag off: no packet object, no listener (pinned).
- **Schema**: `crisis[].label` is removed (not left optional); the wallet card leaves `inserts` and becomes a top-level
  `card` object; the schema's rendering-contract text is rewritten. The schema, data, validator and tests move together
  in this PR in separate commits. If the owner later binds the data to an attestation row, the contract and the data
  can no longer share a PR. **[owner]**

**Interaction**
- Setup blockers whose fix re-arms the gate (unit unconfirmed, callback unconfirmed, ineligible saved listing, missing
  phone) come BEFORE the four confirmations, and a static note beside the mode radios says what a Final copy needs.
- **Clear packet** is shown whenever any saved state exists (listings, unit, profile, mode, page switches) and clears
  all of it and the confirmations; its announcement names what went.
- Any change to the unit's identity (load, unlink, Clear) resets the confirmations; a mode change still keeps them
  (a Review copy is by definition a draft).
- Profile control: a native select labelled "Confirmed unit profile" plus a "Load profile" button (never load on the
  select's change event), read-only name and phone while loaded, a status line, and a "Use my own details instead"
  button that moves focus to the name field. In-place patching covers values, `readOnly`, the status line and the
  draft line.
- **Wallet card:** its control lives in the tray beside Preview (available with no listings), has its own note and
  reason line, and returns focus to itself after printing. Its gate: the payload check and the governed-lines check
  (names and contacts) only; no confirmations, no mode, no unit details.
- The chooser's reason is plain, non-focusable text where the button would be: "Can't be added to the packet: ..."
  with the reason spelled out (crisis services print only in the packet's crisis box; it lists a crisis line number and
  those print only in the crisis box; it has no working phone or website on record). Flag off: markup byte-identical.

**Wallet card (measured)**
- Layout: **two ID-1 panels on one single-sided Letter page**, each with its own dashed cut line. Panel 1: the five
  crisis lines, two-line entries (number over the official governed name), 13px/12px, tight leading, a hairline under
  the title; 194.5px of 204 in every face measured (default, Verdana, Arial). Panel 2: "My numbers", each write-in
  label above two ruled lines; 185.4px. One instruction line under the panels. No availability on the card.
- The panel-1 budget is zero wraps and at most five entries (headroom 9.5px is less than one extra line); the validator
  caps the card at five crisis lines and the browser spec asserts per-entry no-wrap, panel sizes and one page.
- **A metric-stable sans-serif stack for the printed sheet** (Arial, Liberation Sans, Helvetica) so the Mac, the Windows
  ward machines and the CI runner lay out alike; titles use the metric-compatible serif stack. No sizes change.
- The card's crisis-line check covers the governed NAME as well as the contact. Page 1 and the card print the same
  official names.

**Tests**
- One fixture profile in the payload's shipped shape (`tests/fixtures/care-unit-profile.json`, a fictional 555-01xx
  number and a role). Node: passed as the controller's payload. Browser: injected by the route helper. The helper
  `build()` gains a profile option that uses the real Load control. No test ships a real profile.
- Label tests use a sentinel label and compare escaped names; page-count matrices are recorded (4/8/12 listings x both
  copies x week on/off) with the machine and fonts named; a pin on the paper font size catches "shrink to fit"; a node
  pin reads `defaultOn` from the raw JSON; a static guard asserts the packet modules contain no status, closed,
  withdrawn or verification logic.
- Tests that typed a phone to get a Final copy now load the fixture profile (they are not turned into Review-copy
  tests).
