## D1 · Alliance, common factors, rupture & repair — 17 rows

- **41110399** · 2025 · Clin Psychol Rev — *Alliance quality in severe mental illness: SR* — **KEEP (anchor)**
  The closest paper in the queue to the unit's actual census: it maps what helps and hinders alliance formation in psychosis, bipolar, and severe depression. Take-home for an MS3: alliance with SMI patients is buildable, and the levers are partly in the clinician's hands rather than fixed patient traits.

- **41114940** · 2025 · Psychotherapy (Chic) — *Multilevel meta-analysis of client and therapist predictors of alliance* — **GO DEEPER**
  Answers "who makes the alliance — the patient or the therapist?" with the field's best current data, including that therapist effects are real and vary. Better as a resident rail item; the MS3 message is delivered more concretely by 41110399.

- **39930548** · 2025 · Clin Psychol Psychother — *Therapist attachment style and the working alliance* — **CUT**
  Interesting to therapists-in-training, but therapist attachment style is not a variable an MS3 can act on during a six-week rotation. One attachment paper at most belongs in the library, and neither of the two here is core.

- **39086008** · 2025 · Psychother Res — *Adult (client) attachment style and working alliance: updated meta* — **CUT**
  Same verdict as its therapist-side twin: a real literature, but two clicks past what changes MS3 behavior. If a resident asks why some patients warm slowly, point them here informally.

- **39924298** · 2025 · JMIR Ment Health · **OA** — *Does the digital therapeutic alliance exist? Integrative review* — **GO DEEPER**
  A conceptual review of whether "alliance" even translates to apps and chatbots — timely, open access, and a good seminar-discussion piece. Not core: the unit's teletherapy question is answered more directly by the videoconferencing rows.

- **40311538** · 2025 · Clin Psychol Rev — *Assessment instruments for alliance with adolescents* — **CUT**
  Instrument psychometrics in a population the adult unit doesn't serve. Skip unless the child/adolescent service asks for it.

- **39098267** · 2024 · Clin Psychol Rev — *Client and therapist perspectives on alliance in the context of suicidal experiences* — **GO DEEPER ⬇ (verified: proxy-only, meta-ethnography)**
  The alliance paper that touches the unit's hardest task: keeping a relationship alive while assessing suicide risk — verification shows it's a qualitative meta-ethnography behind a proxy wall. Rail item for depth; the same group's OA 2022 review (35168297, below) carries the teaching point as the linkable read.

- **38780549** · 2024 · Psychotherapy (Chic) — *Interpersonal problems and alliance: three-level meta* — **CUT**
  Solid research question (do interpersonally difficult patients form weaker alliances?) but the clinical translation for a student is thin and partly covered by the SMI anchor.

- **38636207** · 2024 · Clin Psychol Rev — *Alliance and outcomes in teletherapy: SR and meta* — **GO DEEPER**
  The most current of the three telehealth-alliance rows. Keep exactly one of {38636207, 36974478, 30014606}; this is the broadest and newest, so if the library wants the modern citation, it's this one — with 30014606 as the highly cited classic alternative.

- **38993343** · 2024 · Front Psychol · **OA** — *Alliance in individual adult psychotherapy: conceptualizations and measures* — **KEEP (on-ramp)**
  The candidate first-read of the domain: an open-access primer on what "alliance" actually means (Bordin's bond/goals/tasks) before students meet the outcome literature. Journal-quality caveat noted — verify it reads cleanly before anchoring the page with it.

- **36974478** · 2024 · J Telemed Telecare — *Alliance in videoconferencing vs in-person psychotherapy* — **CUT**

  The contingency fired exactly as written: same first author (Huggett), open access, 24 citations — this is the linkable read on keeping an alliance alive while assessing suicide risk. ## D2 · Brief interventions on an inpatient unit — 6 rows

- **35997039** · 2023 · Clin Psychol Psychother · **OA** — *Indirect psychological intervention in acute inpatient settings: SR + narrative synthesis* — **KEEP (anchor)**
  "Indirect" means psychology delivered *through the team* — consultation, formulation, staff coaching — which is precisely what an acute unit can actually staff. Take-home for an MS3: psychological care on the unit is mostly a property of the milieu, not a weekly therapy hour; open access seals it.

- **35442174** · 2022 · Psychiatry — *Meta-analysis of psychotherapy in an inpatient setting: moderators of diagnosis and approach* — **KEEP**
  The broad quantitative answer to "does therapy do anything in the average length of stay?", with moderator analysis by diagnosis and modality. Read it for the shape of the evidence, not a single effect size.

- **30520019** · 2019 · Acta Psychiatr Scand — *Efficacy of inpatient psychotherapy for MDD: meta-analysis* — **KEEP**
  The headline an MS3 should carry out of the rotation: structured psychotherapy produces measurable benefit even within an inpatient admission for major depression — the admission is a treatment window, not a holding pattern.

- **26764903** · 2016 · Psychother Psychosom Med Psychol — *[Study quality and treatment outcome in inpatient psychotherapy]* (German) — **CUT**
  German-language and methods-focused; the quality-vs-outcome point is made in English, for a broader audience, in D12.

- **24760412** · 2014 · Psychother Psychosom Med Psychol — *[Premature termination of inpatient psychotherapy]* (German) — **CUT**
  Language barrier again, and dropout is better taught from the D12 dropout cluster.

- **23395190** · 2013 · Am J Geriatr Psychiatry — *Integrated psychological therapy in schizophrenia inpatient settings, by age* — **CUT**
  A narrow, older slice (one manualized program, age-stratified). The domain's honest thinness is better shown by the three keeps than padded with this.
