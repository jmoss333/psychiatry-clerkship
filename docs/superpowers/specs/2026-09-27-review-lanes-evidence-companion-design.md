# Review lanes and evidence companion

## Decision

Daily Review has three transient views: **Clerkship Review** (default), **Landmark Evidence**, and **All due**. Clerkship Review serves Concepts, topic questions, and previously seeded family, communication, and reasoning cards. Landmark Evidence serves the existing AR and SP article questions. All due serves scheduled cards that are due across both lanes, without introducing new cards. Today and timed blocks open All due explicitly. The same `cw_srs_v1` store, card IDs, grades, streak, and global new-card allowance continue across views. No automatic migration or new browser storage key is needed.

## Learner experience

The dashboard presents two visually distinct lane cards with due and available-new counts, plus an All due control. The selected lane governs the dashboard counts and the session queue through one shared predicate. The This week Concept filter applies only in Clerkship Review. A source failing to load produces a visible incomplete count for its lane rather than a false zero. The Today due row shows separate clerkship and landmark shares while retaining its single primary action. Board-style Practice Questions remains its own tool.

After revealing one explicitly paired clinical Concept, a compact diagram connects **Clinical decision → Study result → Important limitation**. The linked article question is an existing CATIE `AR-24#5` card; the clinical card is the existing `CONCEPT#t_psychosis-pearl3:1@2`. The diagram is text and CSS, keyboard and screen-reader readable, hidden before reveal, and offers a link to study the companion in Landmark Evidence. The link grades neither card and preserves the existing session until the learner chooses to leave it. A fresh linked card respects the shared daily new-card limit; a card with an existing schedule can still open. The result and limitation are narrowly selected, verbatim subsets of the reviewed canonical `lieberman-2005-catie` appraisal; the build pins the full fields against drift and an evidence annotation records the paper's supporting words. This new presentation remains pending faculty review and never changes `reviewed.json`.

## Data and governance

Ship a small `tools/review_companions.json` projection on both sites, generated from an explicit pair allowlist and the canonical `evidence_registry.json`. The mapping pins the exact registry fields and is the added teaching input for `review.html`; the built JSON must match the generated bytes. Evidence annotations pass their separate source-span gate without hashing unrelated annotations into Review. The service worker and controlled Review offline pack include the Concepts, article, and companion feeds. Runtime feed validation treats malformed successful responses as unavailable rather than showing false zero cards. Anki packages and existing article quiz IDs remain unchanged.

The original CATIE article has a journal correction recorded by PubMed; the current registry says `none-known`. The correction concerns other outcomes, but faculty must review the correction record and the article question's “primarily” wording before this presentation is treated as approved. The new source-specific claim also requires a primary-text annotation; a passing build is not a clinical sign-off.

## Acceptance

- Existing due schedules, IDs, and shared daily new cap are unchanged.
- A landmark-only due store can be cleared from Today and timed blocks.
- Direct Review opens Clerkship Review; `?lane=all` and `?block=1` open All due.
- The companion result, limitation, and source do not appear in the unrevealed DOM.
- A failed article or companion feed is visibly incomplete; no unsourced clinical text is invented.
- MS3 and resident builds pass static QA, governance dependency checks, and focused browser checks.
