Icon and wayfinding layer · two sites · October 2026

One quiet icon language for two calm sites

A reviewer asked for icons and graphic cues that define the sections and make navigation more intuitive. We treat that as a wayfinding problem. Layout, type, copy and colour stay as shipped. We add one vendored set of line icons, a few "you are here" cues and two layout fixes.

1 · Tell sections apart

Each section and content type gets one icon, always with its visible text label. On Site A, a flashcard deck no longer looks the same as a reading.

2 · Know where you are

The active dock or tab item is shown in teal-deep with a wash pill. Pages get a kicker naming their section: "‹ Library · Evidence & exam" on Site A, "‹ Explore tools · Exercises" on Site B.

3 · Predict a tap

Two trailing marks, used consistently: a chevron means the item opens here, and the external-link icon (with an accessible name) means it opens in a new tab or another site. The whole card becomes the tap target.

Icon set: Lucide, vendored into one registry for both sites

Judged on five criteria: match to rc-icons (24 grid, ~1.8 stroke, round caps), coverage of the two maps, calm tone, licence, and subset size. rc-icons' search, arrow and close glyphs already use Lucide geometry, so Lucide extends the house style rather than replacing it.
