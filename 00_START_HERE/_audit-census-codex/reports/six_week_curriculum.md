# Six-Week Adult Inpatient Psychiatry Clerkship Curriculum

Generated: 2026-06-27

Purpose: a progressive MS3 inpatient psychiatry curriculum that reuses existing material first, while clearly marking what still needs to be created.

## Week 1 - Orientation, Safety, Interviewing, and MSE

**Learning objectives**

- Describe the inpatient unit workflow, student role, supervision structure, and daily expectations.
- Open, structure, and close a psychiatric interview.
- Perform and present a concise mental status exam.
- Recognize when safety concerns require immediate escalation.

**Existing material to mine**

- `Psychiatry_Preceptor_Curriculum/00_Admin/`
- `Psychiatry-Operating-System/execution/Epic_SmartPhrase_Kit.md`
- `Clinical/Teaching/Relational_Psychiatry_Teaching_Manual.docx`
- `Clinical/reconnect-video-content/Scripts/01_Introduction_to_RSS.md` as source material only; translate into plain clinical language.

**Needs creation**

- MS3 orientation packet.
- Daily workflow and rounds one-pager.
- Psychiatric interview pocket guide.
- MSE pocket reference with examples of precise language.

**Clinical skills**

Interview opening, consent/frame-setting, MSE, safety escalation, oral one-liner.

**Assignment**

Write one de-identified MSE and one one-paragraph biopsychosocial summary.

**Estimated time**

3-4 hours outside clinical work.

## Week 2 - Diagnosis, Differential, and Formulation

**Learning objectives**

- Build differentials for psychosis, mania, depression, anxiety, trauma, substance-related symptoms, delirium, catatonia, and personality pathology.
- Use DSM language without reducing the patient to a checklist.
- Create a concise biopsychosocial formulation.

**Estimated time**

## Week 3 - Treatment: Psychopharmacology, Psychotherapy, and Team Care

**Learning objectives**

- Explain first-line inpatient medication classes and common adverse effects.
- Describe supportive psychotherapy and common factors.
- Connect treatment planning to family context, safety, function, and discharge.

## Week 4 - Acute Psychiatry, Consult Thinking, and Legal/Ethical Decisions

**Learning objectives**

- Formulate suicide and violence risk using static, dynamic, protective, and modifiable factors.
- Recognize delirium, catatonia, intoxication, and withdrawal.
- Understand capacity, emergency medication, involuntary care, and consult etiquette.

**Needs creation**
