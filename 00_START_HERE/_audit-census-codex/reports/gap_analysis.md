## Highest-Leverage Gaps

- **Orientation/workflow:** source material exists, but it is not packaged as a six-week MS3 rotation guide.
- **Interviewing/MSE/documentation:** create pocket references and model notes; current resources are indirect.
- **Consult psychiatry:** needs a compact capacity/delirium/catatonia/medical-mimic track.
- **Withdrawal and violence risk:** need explicit inpatient teaching modules.
- **Clinical reasoning/differential diagnosis:** needs worksheet-based practice with synthetic cases.
- **Shelf/OSCE:** needs question sets, stations, rubrics, and oral-presentation drills.

## Strong Existing Pillars

- Family systems and inpatient family engagement materials are unusually strong.
- Patient/family education library has reusable diagnosis-specific handouts.
- Landmark psychiatry summaries and evidence repositories can anchor an article club track.
- ED psychiatry/capstone resources can be adapted for emergency psychiatry, crisis, rights, and discharge teaching.
- ReConnect/RSSM materials are valuable internally, but student-facing versions should use plain clinical language and avoid framework branding unless explicitly desired.
