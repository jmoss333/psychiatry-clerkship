# Shelf/COMAT Blueprint — Gap Fillers

*Added 2026-07-04 — AI-drafted, pending Dr. Moss attestation before use. Supplement to `shelf_review_guide.md` for high-yield topics that are thin elsewhere in the pack.*

**COMAT vs NBME note.** This pack is built COMAT-shaped (behavioral-science emphasis, some ethics/systems framing). The clinical content overlaps heavily with the **NBME Psychiatry shelf**; the differences are format and a few COMAT-only stems (OMM/behavioral-science). Everything below is high-yield on **both**.

## 1. Somatic symptom & related disorders

- **Somatic symptom disorder** — one or more distressing somatic symptoms **plus disproportionate thoughts/feelings/behaviors** about them; symptom may or may not be medically explained. Duration > 6 months.
- **Illness anxiety disorder** — preoccupation with *having/acquiring* a serious illness with **minimal somatic symptoms**; high health anxiety, checking or avoidance.
- **Functional neurological (conversion) disorder** — neurological symptoms (weakness, non-epileptic events, sensory loss) **incompatible with recognized disease** (e.g., Hoover sign, tremor entrainment). Not feigned.
- **Factitious disorder** — falsification/induction of illness **for the sick role** (internal incentive), no external reward.

**Management theme:** one consistent clinician, **scheduled** (not symptom-triggered) visits, validate distress, limit unnecessary work-ups, treat comorbid depression/anxiety, CBT.

## 2. Factitious vs malingering (the classic discriminator)

| | Conscious production? | Motivation | Setting clue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Somatic symptom / FND** | No | None (genuine distress) | Seeks care, wants to get better |
| **Factitious disorder** | **Yes** | **Internal** — to be a patient (sick role) | Eager for tests/procedures; deceptive |
| **Malingering** | **Yes** | **External** — money, drugs, avoiding work/jail | Symptoms tied to a secondary gain; poor cooperation |

Malingering is **not a psychiatric diagnosis** — it's a V-code/coded observation. Suspect it when presentation is inconsistent, incentive is obvious, or cooperation collapses once the reward is off the table.

## 3. Eating disorders + refeeding

- **Anorexia nervosa** — restriction → low weight, intense fear of weight gain, body-image disturbance. Medical: bradycardia, hypotension, hypothermia, QTc, amenorrhea. **No drug is first-line**; nutritional rehab + therapy (FBT in adolescents).
- **Bulimia nervosa** — binge + compensatory purging at normal/above weight; hypokalemia, dental erosion, parotid swelling, Russell sign. **Fluoxetine 60 mg** + CBT.
- **Binge-eating disorder** — binges without compensation; CBT, **lisdexamfetamine** or an SSRI.
- **Refeeding syndrome** — the can't-miss complication of feeding a starved patient: insulin surge drives **phosphate, potassium, and magnesium intracellularly** → **hypophosphatemia** (weakness, arrhythmia, respiratory failure). **Refeed slowly, replete phosphate/K/Mg, give thiamine, monitor electrolytes.**

## 4. Serotonin syndrome vs neuroleptic malignant syndrome (high-yield contrast)

| Feature | Serotonin syndrome | NMS |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Serotonergic drug/combo (SSRI+MAOI, linezolid, tramadol) | Dopamine **blockade** (antipsychotic) or withdrawal of dopamine agonist |
| Onset | **Fast** (< 24 h) | **Slow** (days) |
| Neuromuscular | **Clonus, hyperreflexia**, tremor (lower > upper) | **"Lead-pipe" rigidity**, bradyreflexia |
| Mental status/autonomic | Agitation, hyperthermia, diaphoresis, mydriasis, hyperactive bowel | Stupor, hyperthermia, autonomic instability |
| Labs | ± mild CK | **Markedly ↑ CK**, leukocytosis |
| Management | Stop agent, supportive, benzodiazepines, **cyproheptadine** | Stop antipsychotic, supportive, **dantrolene ± bromocriptine** |

**One-line hook:** fast + clonus/hyperreflexia = **serotonin**; slow + lead-pipe rigidity + sky-high CK = **NMS**. (Compare with **malignant catatonia**, which can be clinically indistinguishable from NMS — treat as an emergency either way.)

## 5. Quick self-check

- A patient eager for repeated surgeries with inconsistent lab findings and no external reward → **factitious disorder**.
- Day 3 of refeeding, new weakness and arrhythmia → check **phosphate** (refeeding).
- SSRI + tramadol, hyperreflexia and clonus within hours → **serotonin syndrome** (cyproheptadine).
- Started haloperidol 3 days ago, now rigid with CK 6000 → **NMS** (stop it; dantrolene).
