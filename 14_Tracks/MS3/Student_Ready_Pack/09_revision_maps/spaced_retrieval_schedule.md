# MS3 Psychiatry — Spaced Retrieval Schedule (Weeks 1–6)

*Added 2026-07-04 — AI-drafted, pending Dr. Moss attestation before use. Companion to `revision_plan.md`.*

**Why this exists.** Rereading feels productive but fades fast. Three things move information into durable memory: **retrieval** (test yourself instead of rereading), **spacing** (revisit at growing intervals), and **interleaving** (mix topics rather than blocking one). This schedule builds all three into the six weeks so you arrive at the shelf/COMAT already over-learned — not cramming.

**The tools do the heavy lifting.** Use the hub's **Daily Review (spaced repetition)** for scheduled recall, **Active Recall (self-test)** for topic drills, and **Shelf Mode** for exam simulation. This page is the cadence that tells you *what* to pull *when*.

## Daily (5 minutes)

Every clinical day, before rounds: answer **3 questions from yesterday's topic** from memory, then check. If you miss one, it comes back tomorrow. That's the whole ritual — it's the single highest-yield 5 minutes in your day.

## Weekly cadence

Each week has a **new focus** plus a **cumulative, interleaved pull** that re-tests earlier weeks at expanding intervals (last week hard, older weeks sampled). Aim for **three ~10-item retrieval sessions per week** (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri), each mixing current + prior material.

| Week | New focus | Interleaved recall (mix into the 10-item pulls) | Checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations: interview, MSE, formulation, safety screen | — (build the base) | — |
| 2 | Mood, psychosis, core psychopharm | W1 safety + MSE (heavy) | — |
| 3 | Psychotherapy, personality | W2 mood/psychosis (heavy) + W1 safety (sample) | **Week-3 self-test (Weeks 1–3)** |
| 4 | Family systems, expressed emotion | W3 personality (heavy) + W2 pharm + W1 formulation (sample) | — |
| 5 | Acute & emergency (suicide, agitation, delirium, withdrawal, catatonia) | W2 psychosis + W1 safety (heavy — safety compounds) + W3–4 (sample) | **Week-5 self-test (Weeks 1–5)** |
| 6 | Integration & exam | **Everything, interleaved** | **Cumulative mock (timed, Shelf Mode)** |

**Reading the table:** "heavy" = 4–5 of the 10 items; "sample" = 1–2 items. The current week always supplies the rest. By Week 5 the safety material has been retrieved 4+ times across expanding gaps — which is exactly why it sticks for the wards and the exam.

## The three checkpoints

- **Week 3 self-test** — 20 items across Weeks 1–3. Anything you miss seeds a daily card. Goal: find the weak spots while there's still time.
- **Week 5 self-test** — 25 items across Weeks 1–5, weighted toward acute/safety. Simulate the pace (≈ 75 sec/item).
- **Week 6 cumulative mock** — a full timed block in **Shelf Mode**; review every miss the same day and re-test the misses 48 h later.

## If you fall behind

Protect the **daily 5-minute pull** and the **three checkpoints** above everything else — they are what convert exposure into recall. Drop the reading before you drop the retrieval.
