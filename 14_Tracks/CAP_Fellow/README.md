# Track — Child & Adolescent Psychiatry Fellow

The adult inpatient spine viewed through a **developmental lens**.

**What this track adds**
- Adolescent depression: **TADS** (fluoxetine + CBT) as the anchor trial.
- Family-focused work is central — adapt the Family Meeting Playbook to caregivers and school systems.
- Developmental differential: neurodevelopmental conditions, trauma, and family system effects.
- Population materials: pediatric/IDD adaptations (`population-adaptations/` in the repo) and perinatal (mother-infant) considerations (`03_Core_Topics/Perinatal`).

**Ordered path:** MS3 spine + the developmental/TADS reading, with every case reframed around the family and developmental stage.
