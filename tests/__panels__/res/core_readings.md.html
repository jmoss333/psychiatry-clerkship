In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
Fourteen hub pages form the spine of the rotation — read these and you will have covered the core diagnostic classes, the acute can't-miss calls, one medication primer, one family/therapy module, and one differential reasoning scaffold.
  • Start with the acute and safety pages before the diagnostic classes — suicide risk, agitation, and consult essentials are the can't-miss calls you will face from Week 1.
  • Two pocket cards belong on your person every day: the Interview & MSE guide and the Formulation & DDx guide — they replace the improvised checklist on rounds.
  • The differential-diagnosis scaffolds and rounds questions pair with the landmark trials audio set for the deepest shelf and COMAT preparation in the hub.

Safety

What to say

Family/collateral
Use readings to sharpen collateral questions: baseline, timeline, adherence, family role, and discharge feasibility.
Rounds
