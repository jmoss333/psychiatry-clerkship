In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
Sequence therapy rationally, recognize genuine first-line failure, escalate to definitive treatment (clozapine, ECT) without delay, and own the safety monitoring that keeps patients alive between doses.
  • "Treatment-resistant" is a measurement claim: confirm adequate dose and duration, verify adherence, and track with a repeated scale before escalating.
  • Two failed adequate antipsychotic trials defines treatment-resistant schizophrenia — clozapine is the only superior agent and is started too late; do not keep cycling.
  • Build monitoring at order entry: lithium renal/thyroid/PTH, clozapine ANC + bowel regimen, antipsychotic metabolic panel, QTc when stacking.
On the unit
On the unit
Treatment Safety Exam
What to ask
Before escalating, verify the measurement claim: adequate dose, adequate duration, adherence, diagnosis, comorbidity, and a repeated symptom scale.
MSE focus
Tie medication changes to target symptoms and observed function, not only the diagnosis name.
Safety
Own monitoring at order entry: ANC, bowel regimen, metabolic labs, QTc, lithium renal/thyroid/PTH, pregnancy considerations, withdrawal risks, and local protocols.
What to say
Explain escalation plainly: what failed, why this next step is different, what monitoring protects against, and what the patient can refuse or ask.
Family/collateral
Ask about prior trials, side effects, adherence barriers, smoking changes, constipation, access, family concerns, and outpatient monitoring feasibility.
Rounds
Present the medication plan as target symptom, evidence rationale, safety monitoring, and contingency.
Exam focus
Know high-yield thresholds and first-line failures: clozapine after two adequate antipsychotic trials, ECT indications, lithium monitoring, serotonin syndrome vs NMS.
Practice medication ambivalence
Test yourself
Test yourself
A patient with schizophrenia has failed two adequate antipsychotic trials with documented adherence. Best next step?
Why: Two failed adequate trials defines treatment-resistant schizophrenia, where clozapine is the only agent with demonstrated superiority; cycling further agents or high-dose combinations just delays the effective treatment.
