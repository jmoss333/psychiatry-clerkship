In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
  • A positive GAD-7 (10 or more) starts a diagnostic interview; it is not a diagnosis.
  • Ask what the fear is about: many things (GAD), attacks (panic), scrutiny (social anxiety), one object (specific phobia).
  • Responders who stop an antidepressant early relapse more often; continue what works.
On the unit
On the unit
Diagnosis Treatment Communication Exam
What to ask
Ask what the worry is about, how long it has lasted, whether it can be controlled, and which of the six symptoms are present.
Safety
Ask about depression, suicidal thoughts, alcohol, cannabis, sedatives and benzodiazepine use.
What to say
Many people with this much worry get real relief; we can start with therapy, a medicine, or both.
Exam focus
GAD: 6 months, hard to control, 3 of 6 symptoms; SSRI or SNRI plus CBT; social anxiety: CBT first; specific phobia: exposure.
Rule out first → first move
Hyperthyroidism Cardiopulmonary causes (arrhythmia, PE) Caffeine or stimulant use Alcohol or sedative withdrawal Akathisia Major depression
First move · Clear the medical and substance mimics, screen for depression, then offer CBT, an SSRI or SNRI, or both by preference and access.

Test yourself
Test yourself
A 34-year-old has 8 months of uncontrollable worry about work, money and family, with poor sleep and muscle tension. Best first-line medication?
Why: SSRIs and SNRIs such as escitalopram, duloxetine and venlafaxine were effective with relatively good acceptability; benzodiazepines and quetiapine were effective but poorly tolerated, and beta-blockers are for performance-only anxiety.
