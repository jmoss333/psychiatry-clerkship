Every psychotropic with a monitoring requirement has a baseline and an ongoing schedule; ordering the labs, β-hCG, and EKG before the first dose and tracking the next level is concrete bedside safety work.
  • Lithium: 12-h trough drawn ~5 days after any dose change; target ≈ 0.6–1.0 (up to ~1.2 in acute mania); NSAIDs/thiazides/ACE-inhibitors raise levels.
  • Clozapine ANC weekly ×6 mo → every 2 wk ×6 mo → monthly (per prescribing information; FDA eliminated the REMS in 2025); myocarditis is the early killer.
  • Valproate and lithium are teratogens — β-hCG before starting; carbamazepine needs CBC/LFTs/sodium, HLA-B*1502 in at-risk ancestry, and watch for SIADH.
