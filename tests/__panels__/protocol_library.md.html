In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
A teaching index of the unit's order sets — always pull exact medications, doses, and monitoring from the live EHR order set; this page tells you which protocol applies and why, not what to prescribe.
  • Withdrawal protocols use symptom-triggered scoring (CIWA-Ar for alcohol/benzo, COWS for opioids); give thiamine before or with glucose in any withdrawal or nutritional risk, never delaying dextrose for hypoglycemia.
  • Clozapine initiation requires mandatory ANC monitoring per the hematologic schedule — know the workflow before the patient starts, not after the first dose.
  • Delirium prevention bundles non-pharmacologic measures first; the restraint and agitation pathway is least-restrictive, time-limited, and always followed by a debrief.
On the unit
On the unit
Treatment Safety Team
What to ask
Identify which protocol applies, what clinical threshold activates it, and what patient factor makes the standard pathway unsafe or insufficient.
MSE focus
Use MSE and bedside findings to support the protocol question: attention for delirium, motor signs for catatonia, arousal for withdrawal, and risk state for safety protocols.
Safety
Do not use this page as an order set; exact medications, doses, monitoring, and escalation steps must come from the live EHR/local policy and supervising clinician.
What to say
I am going to check the current order set and the team plan rather than relying on memory for protocol details.
Family/collateral
Ask nursing, pharmacy, social work, and outpatient clinicians for protocol-relevant history: prior withdrawal severity, clozapine monitoring, safety planning, and discharge barriers.
Rounds
State the protocol, why it applies, the live order set or policy source, and what exception or monitoring issue needs senior review.
Exam focus
Know the conceptual indications and safety logic; local protocol details are not exam facts and should not be guessed.
