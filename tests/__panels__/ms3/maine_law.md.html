In 30 seconds
  • An emergency admission (§3863) is not a commitment; without a staff practitioner's second-opinion certificate within 24 hours, the person must be discharged.
  • Commitment does not authorize treatment over objection; that needs a separate court order under §3864(7-A).
  • A minor may consent to treatment for substance use disorder or emotional or psychological problems (22 §1502), but informal admission under 18 needs a parent or guardian (§3831(3)).
