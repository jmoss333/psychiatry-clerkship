In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
A residency-level reading canon — a Top 25 essential set plus subdomain lists across psychopharmacology, psychotherapy, anxiety/OCD, PTSD, suicide, neuroscience, neuromodulation, addiction, eating disorders, and nosology — used across training, not in four weeks.
  • The Top 25 (STAR*D, CATIE, Cipriani 2018, the ACE study, NCS-R, and more) are the conceptual scaffolding of modern psychiatry.
  • The MS3 site's 50-paper landmark set is the starting subset; this canon extends it to residency-level depth.
  • Use it longitudinally across training — pick a subdomain that matches your current rotation or scholarly interest.

On the unit
Exam Treatment Diagnosis
What to ask
Choose one paper by the patient, rotation, or scholarly question in front of you; do not try to consume the canon linearly.
MSE focus
Use landmark papers to sharpen concepts, then return to the specific patient's observed symptoms, function, and context.
Safety
Do not use historical or trial summaries as local protocol; verify high-risk clinical decisions with current guidelines and supervision.
What to say
Practice explaining a complex evidence idea in one clear sentence before using it with patients, families, or students.
Family/collateral
Let the reading generate better questions about longitudinal course, prior treatment response, family history, trauma, and systems barriers.
Rounds
Bring a canon paper only when it helps the team make a sharper diagnostic, risk, or treatment decision.
Exam focus
Use the Top 25 as a longitudinal board-study spine, then reinforce with active recall and question-bank practice.
