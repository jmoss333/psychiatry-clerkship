In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
Suicide risk assessment is a supervised formulation that turns direct questions, warning signs, means access, and collateral into a safety plan and level-of-care decision.
  • Ask directly about passive death wish, suicidal thoughts, plan, intent, preparation, and access to means.
  • Separate static risk factors, dynamic treatment targets, and acute warning signs.
  • Use safety planning and means safety; do not use no-suicide contracts or scale scores as disposition tools.
On the unit
On the unit
Safety Communication Family Team Exam
What to ask
Ask directly about ideation, plan, intent, preparation, past attempts, access to means, protective factors, and what has changed today.
MSE focus
Track hopelessness, agitation, insomnia, intoxication, psychosis, impulsivity, cognition, and ability to collaborate.
Safety
Formulate acute vs chronic risk, means access, warning signs, observation needs, discharge barriers, and escalation triggers with the supervising team.
What to say
I ask directly about suicide because it helps us keep you safe; you will not shock me by answering honestly.
Family/collateral
With permission or safety exception, ask about means, recent behavior, prior attempts, supervision, and the discharge environment.
Rounds
Present risk state, risk status, drivers, means access, protective factors, collateral, and the supervised plan.
Exam focus
Direct questions do not implant suicidality; safety plans replace no-suicide contracts, and risk tools do not decide disposition.
