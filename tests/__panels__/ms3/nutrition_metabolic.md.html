In 30 seconds
In 30 seconds
Track metabolic health for every patient on an antipsychotic — weight monthly for the first 3 months then quarterly, glucose and lipids at baseline and 12 weeks then per schedule — and know the dietary safety points for lithium, MAOIs, and grapefruit/CYP3A4.
  • Olanzapine and clozapine carry the highest metabolic burden; aripiprazole, lurasidone, ziprasidone, and cariprazine carry the lowest — choose by side-effect profile when efficacy is comparable.
  • Lithium levels shift with sodium and hydration changes; MAOI patients must avoid tyramine-rich foods (aged cheeses, cured meats, tap beer) to prevent hypertensive crisis.
  • A Mediterranean dietary pattern is the best-supported food-as-treatment signal for depression (SMILES trial); pair dietary counseling with Motivational Interviewing for sustainable change.

On the unit
Treatment Safety Communication Team Exam
What to ask
Ask weight trajectory, appetite, food insecurity, eating patterns, activity, diabetes/lipid history, medication concerns, hydration/salt swings, and access to follow-up labs.
MSE focus
Notice sedation, activation, negative symptoms, binge/purge clues, body image distress, cognition, and motivation or ambivalence around change.
Safety
Check antipsychotic metabolic monitoring, lithium hydration/sodium risks, MAOI tyramine counseling, grapefruit/CYP interactions, food insecurity, and eating-disorder red flags.
What to say
Metabolic health is part of psychiatric care, especially when the medication that helps symptoms can also change weight, glucose, or lipids.
Family/collateral
Ask family or outpatient clinicians about baseline weight, shopping/cooking access, medication adherence, activity, food insecurity, and lab follow-up barriers.
Rounds
Present metabolic baseline, medication risk, labs ordered, nutrition/activity supports, and follow-up responsibility after discharge.
Exam focus
Olanzapine and clozapine carry the highest metabolic burden; antipsychotic monitoring includes weight, glucose/A1c, lipids, and blood pressure.

First move · Document baseline weight, fasting glucose, and lipid panel before starting any antipsychotic; counsel MAOI patients on tyramine; check sodium and hydration with lithium; schedule the 12-week recheck at the time of prescribing.

Test yourself
Test yourself
A patient started on olanzapine three months ago had weight, glucose, and lipids checked at baseline. Nothing has been rechecked. What monitoring is now overdue?
Why: ADA/APA consensus monitoring recommends checking weight, fasting glucose, and fasting lipids at approximately 12 weeks after starting an antipsychotic — this is the first scheduled interval recheck, not an annual event. Rechecking weight only is incomplete; a full endocrine panel is not the standard; waiting a full year misses early metabolic drift.
