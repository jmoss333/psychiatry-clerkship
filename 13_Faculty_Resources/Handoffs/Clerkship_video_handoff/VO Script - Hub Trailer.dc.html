Recording notes
  • Record each cue as its own take, plus one full straight-through pass. Leave a half-second of silence before and after every line.
  • Small room, soft surfaces; phone or USB mic 6–8 inches away, just off-axis. A closet full of clothes beats an office.
  • Pace: the timecodes have slack built in. If a line runs long, cut words rather than speeding up — the cut breathes at ~125 wpm.
  • Do two alternates of the opening and closing lines — those set and seal the tone.
  • Sync anchors are approximate: match the feel of each beat, and we'll nudge the edit to the read, not the reverse.
