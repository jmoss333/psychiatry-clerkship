- [ ] **Lithium first-trimester cardiac malformation** — adjusted RR ~1.65 (≈2.4% vs 1.2%); dose-dependent (>900 mg RR ~3.22). Risk–benefit: NNT ~3 to prevent relapse vs NNH ~33 for a cardiac anomaly. Fetal echo at 16–20 wk. *(Patorno, NEJM 2017; Fornaro, AJP 2020)*
- [ ] **Valproate** — ~9.3% major malformation, neural-tube/spina bifida 1–2%, mean IQ reduction ~8–11 points, autism ~2.9×. Avoid in people who may become pregnant; switch to lamotrigine. *(NEAD; AAN/AES/SMFM 2024)*
- [ ] **Lamotrigine PK trap** — clearance rises up to ~230% by ~week 32 (dose up in pregnancy), then cut ~25%/week postpartum to avoid toxicity. *(MONEAD, Pennell 2022)*
- [ ] **Paroxetine** — cardiac malformation OR ~1.28; prefer sertraline/escitalopram. PNAS (poor neonatal adaptation) in ~20–30%, self-limited. *(ACOG 2023; Huybrechts, NEJM 2014)*
- [ ] **Lactation RIDs** — sertraline 0.4–2.2% (first-line); quetiapine/olanzapine preferred antipsychotics; **lithium RID 12–30%** — if used, monitor infant lithium level, TSH, creatinine. *(review-cited; confirm against LactMed)*
