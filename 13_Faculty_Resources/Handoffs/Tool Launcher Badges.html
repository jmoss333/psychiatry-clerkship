Alcohol withdrawal — recognition & scoring

Escalating autonomic arousal — tremor, sweats, tachycardia, and rising anxiety within 6–24 hours of the last drink — should prompt formal, repeated scoring rather than eyeballing. Score at the bedside with the %%ICON:withdrawal%%CIWA-Ar / COWS tool and treat symptom-triggered. For suspected opioid withdrawal the same tool carries COWS.

Because withdrawal severity and safety travel together, keep the risk screens close at hand: an unsafe patient may need the %%ICON:cssrs%%C-SSRS before discharge planning, and a documented %%ICON:capacity%%Decisional Capacity note if the patient wants to leave against advice.

Practically, that means scoring on a schedule rather than by impression. Reach for the %%ICON:withdrawal%%CIWA-Ar / COWS at fixed intervals, chart the trend, and let the trajectory — not a single number — drive escalation or spacing of doses.

Comorbid mood and anxiety symptoms are easy to over-attribute to withdrawal in the first 48 hours. Re-measure once the patient is stable with the %%ICON:screeners%%PHQ-9 / GAD-7 before attributing a primary disorder, and document the moving parts cleanly with the %%ICON:mse%%Mental Status Exam.

When you present this patient on rounds, lead with the trend and the plan, not the raw list of symptoms. A tight, timed pitch — rehearsed with the %%ICON:oral%%Rounding Prep + Timer — and the shared taper logic in the %%ICON:decision-aids%%Decision Aids keep the team aligned. Notice the launcher stays pinned bottom-right as you scroll — always reachable, never covering the text.

Copy-paste snippets

Two forms. In each you change only data-tool (and the matching href/aria-label/label). Paste the shared style block and script once per page (or keep them in your global stylesheet + app bundle). Swap the inline <svg> for the tool's glyph from the gallery above.

A · Inline chip

Best for a single, obviously-relevant tool sitting next to a passage. Add is-safety for the terracotta emphasis on C-SSRS / Violence, or is-compact for tighter text.

B · Floating dock

Best when a page maps to 2–3 tools. Drop one dock per page and list its badges; the FAB stays out of the way until tapped.

Shared · style + routing script (once per page)

The routing script makes the same badge work everywhere: it opens the tool in-app via the sidebar if present, posts to the parent if embedded in an iframe, and otherwise falls back to the ?tool= link. No framework, no build step.

Placement guide, per tool, is printed on each card in the gallery above (“Lives on…”). The safety variant is reserved for C-SSRS and Violence Risk so those two read as urgent at a glance.
