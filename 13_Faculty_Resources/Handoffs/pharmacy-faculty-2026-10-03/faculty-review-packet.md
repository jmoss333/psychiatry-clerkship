## Remaining uncertainties and boundaries

- Scope is deliberately limited to the selected products. A statement that another formulation exists is not its administration guidance; excluded formulations require separate sources and review.
- Naltrexone source configuration now explicitly selects ORAL and removes the VIVITROL brand preference. The actual oral reference is retained and no injectable claim is made. A future injection card needs its own route/source configuration and review.
- Preserved binary monitoring flags are upstream carried facts; added monitoring text explicitly rejects treating them as exemptions. These flags do not replace a risk-based clinical plan.
- Comparative efficacy/weight rankings and treatment-strategy assertions removed here need separate evidence before reinstatement. Existing untouched evidence-based pearls remain for faculty review.
- Existing evidence/question/monitoring links resolve; a broad question or teaching page is not drug-specific approval. New-card empty interaction lists are intentional because matching interaction-card IDs do not exist.
- Label verification and readability checks do not establish clinical correctness or attest a question mapping. The console approval workflow is separate work; this branch never manufactures its hashes.
- Source receipt dates distinguish selected-label retrieval from multi-label agreement checks. See independent-label-check.json for all sixteen FDA/DailyMed URLs and digests; see ten-card-revision.json for exact before/after fields.
