WHY THIS CAPTURE EXISTS
-----------------------
A Creative Commons BY-NC licence is an affirmative, third-party grant: anyone may
reuse the publication for non-commercial purposes with attribution. It is the basis
on which standards.json records this framework's rights as `granted` and its six
competency names as reproducible. A web licence statement can change without notice,
so this dated capture — not the live URL — is the record of what the source said on
the date the claim was made. (research_returns.json invariant 2, web variant.)

The project posture is still PARAPHRASE ONLY for subcompetency and competency-
statement text (curriculum-architecture remediation handoff, WP-17). The licence
would permit verbatim non-commercial reuse with attribution; the choice not to use
it is ours, not a restriction. The colophon names "CC BY-NC" without a version
number; which version applies is recorded as an open question in standards.json.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

six competencies and publishes NO alphanumeric codes. Its only identifiers are the
competency name plus a plain number restarting within each competency, e.g.
"Systems-Based Practice 4". The FC-PROF ... FC-SBP codes in standards.json are a
LOCAL convention of this repository and must be labelled as such wherever cited.
