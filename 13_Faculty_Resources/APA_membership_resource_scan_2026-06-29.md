# APA Membership Resource Scan

Date: 2026-06-29  
Scope: American Psychiatric Association and APA Publishing resources that could strengthen the psychiatry clerkship library.

Access note: This scan used public APA, APA Publishing, PsychiatryOnline, and APA Learning Center metadata. I did not log into a personal APA account. Items marked "APA login" should be checked while signed in. Items marked "APA Publishing / PsychiatryOnline" may require a separate subscription or purchase, although APA members receive discounted pricing through appi.org.

## Mapping to Current Gaps

### Needs expansion

- Consult psychiatry: Study Guide to Consultation-Liaison Psychiatry; Clinical Manual of Psychopharmacology in the Medically Ill; Textbook of Hospital Psychiatry; Collaborative Care training.
- Capacity: APA psychiatric evaluation guideline material, hospital psychiatry texts, integrated care/consult resources. No single obvious APA "capacity pocket guide" surfaced in the public metadata.
- Delirium: Textbook of Hospital Psychiatry; medically ill psychopharmacology manual; geriatrics study guide; APA Learning Center older adult cognition courses.
- Catatonia: PsychiatryOnline and Learning Center search should be checked inside the account; no strong public APA catalog hit found in this scan.
- Withdrawal/SUD: Study Guide to Substance Abuse Treatment; Pharmacotherapy for Complex Substance Use Disorders; Learning Center polysubstance use material.
- Student documentation and oral presentations: DSM-5-TR diagnostic exam/pocket guide; Study Guide to Introductory Psychiatry; AJP Residents' Journal examples.

### Needs creation

- MS3 orientation packet: add APA medical student page, medical student programs, residency application guide, and AJP Residents' Journal.
- Interview/MSE pocket guide: DSM-5-TR, Pocket Guide to the DSM-5-TR Diagnostic Exam, Study Guide to Introductory Psychiatry.
- Week-by-week reading map: use APA/PsychiatryOnline study guides as optional faculty-curated readings, not required student reading unless access is confirmed.
- OSCE cases: DSM self-exam questions and DSM-5-TR diagnostic exam materials can inform diagnostic distractors and scoring anchors.
- Shelf guide: Study Guide to Introductory Psychiatry, Study Guide to Psychiatry, DSM-5-TR Self-Exam Questions.
- Synthetic practice cases: DSM-5-TR diagnostic structure plus APA patient/family pages for plain-language framing.

## Source Notes

- APA Learning Center: integrated education/CME home; public metadata says 200+ activities, with member pricing advantages.
- APA Publishing member benefits: appi.org member sign-in unlocks member discounts.
- PsychiatryOnline subscription contents: includes DSM, self-exam questions, study guides, textbooks, patient guides, psychotherapy texts, and hospital/medically ill psychiatry content.
- APA patient/family pages and La Salud Mental are public-facing and useful for discharge/family education.
- SMI Adviser national version is described by APA as having run from 2018 to 2024; current APA SMI workforce examples include SMI CalAdviser.
