## The five-minute observation

1. **Before (30 seconds).** Ask which card and which rows the student wants watched. A Sim-to-Ward
   encounter card names the rows for you.
2. **During (3 minutes or more).** Watch one segment (an opening, a safety inquiry, a mental status
   examination, one presentation, one family conversation) and write down the student's actual
   words. Step in at once if safety requires it; the observation then becomes supervision.
3. **After (2 minutes).** Rate only the rows you saw; "not observed" is a complete answer. Rate the
   task on this day, not the student: level 4, *ready for indirect supervision*, does not change
   what an MS3 may do alone. Give feedback, then sign.

## What to say

Use the two models in [Supervision, EPAs & Teaching the Student](../../14_Tracks/Resident/supervision_teaching.md)
on the resident site, which this guide does not repeat: the **One-Minute Preceptor** for case
discussion and **Ask–Tell–Ask** for observed skills. Applied to a card, and saying when you move
from feedback (formative) to evaluation (summative):

- **Ask** which row went best and which the student would redo.
- **Tell** one behavior you saw, in the student's own words, and one concrete change for that row.
- **Ask** what they will do at the next encounter; they write it on the plan line.
- **Write** the narrative box about behavior, not personality: it is what LCME element 9.5 asks for.
