## Required elements

Mark each element: **P** present and adequate · **I** incomplete · **M** missing.

| # | Element (what adequate looks like) | P | I | M | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identification and chief concern.** Who the patient is clinically and why they came, in the patient's words where possible. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 2 | **History of present illness with a timeline.** Onset, course, precipitants, and why now; the reader can see the sequence. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 3 | **Safety assessment with a risk formulation.** Suicide and violence risk with chronic and acute factors separated, protective factors, modifiable targets, and means access. "Denies SI/HI, low risk" alone is incomplete. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 4 | **Substance, medication, and medical review.** Contributors considered, what was checked, and what is still pending. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 5 | **Mental status examination, internally consistent.** Agrees with the history and with itself (for example, stated mood against observed affect, and thought content against what the history reports). Uses observable language. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 6 | **Differential diagnosis including medical mimics.** Medical, substance- or medication-induced, and neurocognitive causes considered; the leading diagnosis is justified; the formulation says why this person, why now. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 7 | **Plan by problem.** Safety and observation level; medications and their monitoring; medical workup; therapy and milieu; family and collateral; disposition. The plan follows from the formulation. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 8 | **Clarity and concision.** Reasoning is visible; no copied-forward text; length fits the case; a covering clinician could act on it. | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |

## Overall rating for writing an admission note

Rate what the student needed from you for this note. The scale is the pack's own wording.

| Level | Label | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Needs direct supervision | You had to rewrite essential sections or supply the reasoning. |
| 2 | Needs prompting | The note became adequate after your specific prompts on one or more essential elements. |
| 3 | Needs occasional checking | Essential elements present and sound; you would still check selected parts before co-signing. |
| 4 | Ready for indirect supervision | Accurate, safe, and complete; you could review it after the fact rather than line by line. |

**Overall:** ☐ needs direct supervision ☐ needs prompting ☐ needs occasional checking ☐ ready
for indirect supervision
