## Why these five items

The five items below are quoted verbatim from the optional self-check in the library's
Treatment Team Rounding Prep tool (`oral.html`). Students tap the same five items there after a
practice run, so their self-check and your rating use one vocabulary and can be compared line by
line. Do not reword them here: `tests/assessment-pack.test.mjs` reads the tool's source and fails
if this card and the tool drift apart.

The fifth item is stored in the tool under the key `endedWithAsk`, but its label reads "End with a
plan or disposition thought." Rate it as the label reads: a plan, a disposition thought, or a
specific question for the team all count.

**How to use it (the presentation itself plus 2 minutes).** Listen to one presentation and mark
your column. Immediately afterward, and before you say anything, the student marks the
self-check column for the same presentation. Then compare the two columns together.

## Overall rating for presenting on rounds

Rate what the student needed from you for this presentation. The scale is the pack's own wording.

| Level | Label | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Needs direct supervision | You had to reorganize the presentation or supply the assessment and plan. |
| 2 | Needs prompting | The team understood the patient only after your questions filled an essential gap. |
| 3 | Needs occasional checking | Organized and accurate; you would still check selected facts or the plan. |
| 4 | Ready for indirect supervision | Concise, accurate, and safe; the team could act on it without follow-up questions. |
