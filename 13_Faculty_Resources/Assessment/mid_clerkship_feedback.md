# Mid-Clerkship Feedback Meeting

**DRAFT for Josh's edit.** Formal formative feedback at the **end of Week 3** of the six-week
clerkship (the D3 spine places it there). About 20–30 minutes.

**Why the end of Week 3.** LCME element 9.7 (Formative Assessment and Feedback), in the 2026–27
*Functions and Structure of a Medical School*, states:

> "Formal feedback occurs at least at the midpoint of the course or clerkship."

In a six-week clerkship the end of Week 3 is the midpoint, and it leaves three weeks to act on
the targets. Review §6 lists a mid-clerkship self-assessment against objectives as a Tier 1 gap.

> This form feeds your school's official evaluation; it is not itself the grade. It is feedback,
> not evaluation: its job is to set up the second half of the rotation. Record no patient
> information of any kind (patient initials: not recorded; encounter type only, where needed).
> Submit it to [your school's evaluation system], never to this library.

**Before the meeting.** The student completes section 1 and section 4 and brings any completed
direct-observation cards (DO-1 to DO-4, and encounter cards). The preceptor may ask residents,
nurses, and social workers for input first.

**In the meeting.** Start with the student's self-rating (Ask), give your view and the two
targets (Tell), and end with the student's plan (Ask). The feedback models are in
[preceptor_guide.md](preceptor_guide.md).
