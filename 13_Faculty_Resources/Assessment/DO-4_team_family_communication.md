# DO-4 · Team and Family Communication

**DRAFT for Josh's edit.** Direct-observation card with four variants. Spine Week 5 (Risk,
rights and disposition) for the family meeting or safety-planning conversation; Week 4 for the
collateral call; handoffs and post-event debriefs whenever they happen.

**This card can be completed by a nurse, social worker, occupational therapist, or peer support
specialist** who watched the whole conversation. A rating from a non-physician team member is
this pack's evidence for Core EPA 9; review §6 found EPA 9 never assessed.

**EPA:** Core EPA 9 (Collaborate as a Member of an Interprofessional Team), all variants; Core EPA 1 and Core EPA 6 (variant B); Core EPA 8 (variant C); Core EPA 13 (variant D)

**Objectives:** OBJ-11, OBJ-12, OBJ-16, OBJ-06 (variant A), OBJ-13 (variants A and C), OBJ-10 (variant B), OBJ-01 (variant C), OBJ-15 (variant D)

**LCME:** 9.4 (direct observation); 9.5 (the narrative box)

## Rating scale

Rate what the student needed from you **for this conversation, on this day**. The scale is the
pack's own wording; it is not a published instrument.

| Level | Label | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Needs direct supervision | You or another team member had to lead the conversation or step in on essential parts. |
| 2 | Needs prompting | The student carried the conversation once cued; at least one essential part needed a prompt. |
| 3 | Needs occasional checking | The student carried the essential parts unprompted; someone would still check selected parts afterward. |
| 4 | Ready for indirect supervision | Clear and safe without cues; the team could rely on the student's summary afterward. |

  (`rp-post-event-huddle.html`): the patient, team communication, and system conditions. An MS3

  still asks each student to self-rate OBJ-15.

  is the school's decision.
