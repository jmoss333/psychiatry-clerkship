**How to use it (5 minutes of preceptor time).** The student names the rows they want watched.
Observe a segment of a real interview (the opening, the safety inquiry, or the mental status
examination is enough) without taking over unless safety requires it. Rate only the rows you saw;
"not observed" is a complete answer. Then give feedback with Ask–Tell–Ask (see
[preceptor_guide.md](preceptor_guide.md)).

## Rating scale

Rate what the student needed from you **for this task, on this day**. The scale is the pack's own
wording; it is not a published instrument.

| Level | Label | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Needs direct supervision | You had to stay with the student throughout and supply or redo essential parts. |
| 2 | Needs prompting | The student completed the task once cued; at least one essential part needed your prompt. |
| 3 | Needs occasional checking | The student completed the essential parts unprompted; you would still verify selected parts afterward. |
| 4 | Ready for indirect supervision | Accurate and safe without cues; you could review the result afterward instead of watching it. |

Level 4 is a statement about this task on this day. It does not change what the student may do
alone: MS3 supervision follows the school's and the site's rules.
