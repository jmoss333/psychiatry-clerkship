## How to use it

1. Install Anki (desktop is free; **AnkiMobile** on iOS / **AnkiDroid** on Android is free on Android).
2. Open the downloaded `.apkg` — it imports as **Psychiatry Clerkship Library (Moss)** with the two subdecks.
3. **Suspend everything, then unsuspend by topic** as the rotation covers each block. Browse by subdeck and source tags to select material for your current block.
4. Cap new cards around 20–30/day and review daily — the schedule does the rest.

**Pair with** — the [Practice Questions tool](?tool=question-bank-practice.html) for timed, exam-style practice of the same items, and the [COMAT & Shelf Review](?page=shelf.md) guide for the blueprint.

**Current source review** — Concepts are included only while their source pages retain effective review for both sites. Decks refresh and are checked against the site’s released cards on each build. Source review and technical checks do not replace faculty review of changed card faces.
