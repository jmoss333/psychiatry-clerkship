Differential Decision Trees

Eight core inpatient syndromes. For each: rule out the medical can't-miss first, then run the psychiatric differential, then make the first move. Tap a chip to read it; everything is a teaching scaffold, not a substitute for supervised assessment.

The one rule that prevents most errors: a new or acutely changed psychiatric presentation is a medical workup until proven otherwise — vitals, glucose, basic labs, tox, and a focused neuro exam come before you anchor on a psychiatric diagnosis, especially in the old, the medically ill, and the first episode.

How to present a differential on rounds (10 seconds)

“My leading diagnosis is X because [2 features]; I’m also considering Y and Z; and I want to rule out [the medical can’t-miss] with [test].”
