Before you begin

You are facilitating a voluntary inpatient family meeting after Morgan's medical stabilization. Morgan lives alone. Start by introducing yourself and hearing what each person hopes to discuss.

Both agree to talk about their concerns and possible support. Brief separate check-ins are allowed in this fictional scenario. Private dialogue stays in its own view when you rejoin; it is never automatically disclosed to the other person.

Fictional practice only. Starting enables your microphone; your browser may send audio to its recognition service.
