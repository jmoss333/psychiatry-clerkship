The family-side companion to Recovery Companion. Two weeks of structured practice. One bridge into CSC.

After the 72-hour meeting, families need somewhere to keep practicing the skills they were introduced to. Family Communication Coach is the family member's daily, web-based scaffold for the first 14 days post-discharge: micro-exercises, communication scripts, what-to-watch-for cues, and structured check-ins that flow into the warm handoff to CSC family services.

Day 3 of 14 · Communication Practice
Today's skill: Permission asking
Why this skill matters
Heavy conversations need consent first
Asking "Is now a good time to talk about something hard?" before raising a difficult topic reduces defensiveness on both sides. It's small. It changes everything.

One skill per day
Twelve micro-skills across 14 days
Each skill maps to a modality introduced during the 72-hour meeting. Days 13–14 are integration and handoff. Total time per day: 5–8 minutes.
Scripts & scaffolds
Specific language, not just principles
"Try saying X" rather than "communicate clearly." The skills include exact opening sentences, repair phrases, and de-escalation scripts.
Watch-for cues
Family-side early-warning anchors
Each day surfaces 2–3 patient-specific early-warning signs the family practiced naming in the meeting — so families recognize them rather than minimizing or escalating.
Crisis pathways
When to call, when to listen
Explicit thresholds and named contact paths — the family safety plan from the 72-hour meeting, surfaced in context when relevant cues appear.

Why this matters for the CSC engagement gap

74–84% of CSC families have contact with the treatment team; 22–46% participate in evidence-based family intervention (Oluwoye FAMES; Cohen et al. Massachusetts 2026). Most of the gap opens during the discharge → CSC window. Family Communication Coach is designed to keep families practicing engaged behaviors during the highest-risk window, so they arrive at CSC having already used the skills the outpatient team will reinforce. The tool also emits a structured "skills-practiced" log for the warm handoff PDF — giving the receiving team specific evidence of what the family has already worked on.
