# Learner Pipeline — Context

## Annual volume

| Source | Volume | Block length | Year coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| MMC general psychiatry residency | 4 / year | 4 weeks each | ~16 weeks |
| UNE COM medical school | Continuous (~8 students rotating yearly) | 6 weeks each | ~52 weeks |

## Implications for the system
- **Always at least one learner.** Often two simultaneously (resident + MS overlap).
- **MS is the continuity learner.** They're there for 6 weeks and may overlap with up to 1.5 resident blocks.
- **Resident is the deep-dive rotator.** Higher expectations, deeper formulation work, primary-source citations, independent family meetings.
- **Friday didactic = joint** because both learners are usually there.
- **Supervision = 1:1** to protect privacy and depth.
