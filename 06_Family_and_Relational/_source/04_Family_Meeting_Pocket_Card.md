# Family Meeting Pocket Card
### Moss Family Meeting Framework · carry on the unit
*(Print 2-up double-sided from the companion `04_Pocket_Card_printable.html`, then laminate.)*

---

## ▣ SIDE A — RUN THE MEETING

**The one idea:** *No one listens unless they feel heard.*
**Where it sits:** RSS **Layer 3** — only after biological + regulatory stability.

**0 PREPARE** — Safe (IPV screen, private) · team aligned · ONE goal · co-facilitator
**1 OPEN** — purpose · "people see it differently — that's useful" · one voice, no blame
**2 LISTEN** — *feel-heard first* · "What I hear you saying is…" · feeling before facts
**3 TRACK THE DANCE** — name the cycle, not the villain · "Does that match?"
**4 REACH THE FEELING** — secondary → primary · "Under the anger, I hear fear"
**5 REFRAME** — function & meaning · externalize the illness · re-story the admission
**6 RESPECT LINES** — "When ___ / I feel ___ / I need ___" + **one moment of contact**
**7 PLAN** — one small relational task · next meeting · each person's next step
**8 CLOSE LOOP** — document · debrief team · hand off to outpatient

## ▣ SIDE B — SCRIPTS & SAFETY

**OPENING SCRIPT**
*"Thanks for coming. We're here to understand how things have been, share what we've learned, and start planning for home. I'll ask each of you — there are no wrong answers, and people often see things differently. That's useful, not a problem. One voice at a time; we're here to understand, not blame."*

**CIRCULAR QUESTIONS**
• "When he goes quiet, what do you do — then what does he do?"
• "Who worries most out loud? Who worries silently?"
• "If you stopped doing X, what are you most afraid would happen?"
• "Before the illness vs. now — who's closer to whom?"

**MENTALIZATION PROMPTS**
• "What do you imagine she was feeling right then?"
• "What story did you tell yourself about what that meant?"

**VALIDATION**
• "That sounds frightening." • "That makes sense given what you've been carrying." • "You can't feel that much anger unless you care."

**REFRAMES**
• "The protecting comes from love — and it's also removing the consequence."
• "His refusal may be protecting the family from facing how much needs to change."
• "Coming in was something you did together — that's love working."

**RESPECT LINE** → "When [situation], I feel [emotion], I need [request]."
**REGULATION** → "I'm at 40% today." · "Can I vent for a minute?" · safe word + return time.

**CLOSING SCRIPT**
*"Here's the one thing to try this week: [specific relational task]. We'll meet again [when]. Progress, not perfection — small changes make big shifts."*

**PITFALLS** ✗ giving advice before they feel heard ✗ blaming a person instead of naming the pattern ✗ opening more emotion than time can hold ✗ skipping the IPV screen ✗ carrying messages (triangulation) ✗ doing Layer-3 work on a Layer-1 brain.

**STOP / DON'T MEET** active or severe IPV · ongoing family abuse · autonomous refusal · not yet stable.
